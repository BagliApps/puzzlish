Support

Found a bug or a wrong meaning, or have an idea? Write to walletude1@gmail.com, or use Settings › Send feedback in the app, which adds your device and app version for you. If it is about a word, please include the word and the mode.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play a mode?
Tap the ⓘ button at the top of any puzzle for a short animated guide.

What are coins for?
Hints: a letter, the whole word or, in some modes, the meaning. You earn coins by finishing levels, and you can watch an optional ad for more.

Will I lose my progress when I update?
No. Your progress stays on your device across updates. Deleting the app deletes it.

I bought "Remove ads" on another device.
Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account and open Settings › Shop and tap Restore purchases.

Can I change the language?
Settings › Language. Each language has its own levels, stats and word book; coins are shared.

How do I change my ad consent?
Settings › Privacy and ad choices. On iPhone and iPad, tracking permission is in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Where do the words come from?
From open data such as Wiktionary, Tatoeba, the CEFR-J Wordlist and FrequencyWords. The full list with licences is in Settings › Sources and licences.
