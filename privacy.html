Privacy Policy

Puzzlish · Effective date: 27 October 2026

Puzzlish is a word-puzzle game for learning English vocabulary, made by Alper Bağlı ("we"). It has no accounts and no servers of our own. It is free and supported by ads, so some data is handled by the advertising, purchase and crash-report services listed below. This policy explains what they are and how you control them.

What stays on your device

Your levels, stats, My Word Book, coins, unfinished puzzles and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them; Settings › Reset progress clears most of them. Like other app data, they may be included in your device's iCloud or Google backup, which is run by Apple or Google under their own policies.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Puzzlish shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and rewarded ads you choose to watch for coins. To show and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect:

  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country or city level, never precise GPS location);
  • a device advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone and iPad, Advertising ID on Android) and app-scoped identifiers;
  • which ads were shown, taps and other ad interactions;
  • device and app performance data, and crash data from the ad SDK.

Google uses this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required). On first launch Puzzlish asks for your consent through Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy and ad choices. If you do not consent, Puzzlish shows no ads at all.
  • Tracking on iPhone and iPad. Puzzlish asks for permission to track only after the ad consent step. If you decline, Google does not get the advertising identifier (IDFA). You can change this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads).
  • Remove ads. The in-app "Remove ads" purchase turns off banner and full-screen ads. Rewarded ads stay available only if you choose to watch them.

Purchases (RevenueCat)

Purchases (remove ads, coin packs) are paid through the App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. To check and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase history together with a random anonymous ID created by the app. We do not send your name, email or any other identifier to RevenueCat. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Crash reports (Sentry)

If the app crashes, an anonymous error report (device model, system and app version, and the technical details of the error) is sent to Sentry so that we can fix it. Reports contain no personal data, no screenshots and no IP-based user profile. You can turn this off in Settings › Send crash reports. See the Sentry Privacy Policy.

Feedback

If you send feedback from Settings, your own email app opens with a message to us. We receive only what you send, use it only to answer you and to improve the app, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Retention and your rights

We do not keep a copy of any data about you. AdMob, RevenueCat and Sentry keep data for the periods described in their policies. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or KVKK), you have rights to access, correct or delete your data and to object to its processing. Because we hold no data linked to you, the quickest way is to use the choices above or to contact the service concerned; you can also write to us and we will help.

Children

Puzzlish is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date above.
